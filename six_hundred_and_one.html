The serial · Season one

Six hundred people wake in a room that should not exist. Otto is forced to make choices no one should have to make. But the room has rules, and someone has already decided how this story ends. The question is whether Otto is a prisoner—or the architect.

About the story

Six Hundred and One is a serialized psychological thriller about control, guilt, memory, and the stories people tell themselves to survive.

Episodes are intended to be around 500–600 words and published twice a week. The truth behind the room will unfold gradually.
